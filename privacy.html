Privacy Policy

Simple information about how Pet Care Buddy handles your data.

1. About Your Data

Pet Care Buddy is designed as a simple pet-care web application. The current version stores pet profiles, reminders and contact form information locally in your browser using local storage.

2. Information You Add

Depending on the features you use, you may enter information such as:

  • Pet name and basic pet details
  • Pet-care reminders
  • Care-related notes
  • Feedback or contact form information

3. Local Storage

The current app uses your browser's local storage for certain features. This means the information can remain on the device and browser where you entered it.

4. Your Control

You can remove locally stored application information by clearing the website's stored browser data. If you clear that data, saved pet profiles and reminders may be removed.

5. Future Changes

As Pet Care Buddy develops, new features may change how information is handled. This page can be updated when new data-related features are introduced.

💜 Important: Pet Care Buddy is a project in development. Do not enter highly sensitive or confidential personal information into the current version.
