About Pet Care Buddy

Pet Care Buddy is a simple digital companion designed to help pet parents organize everyday pet care in one friendly place.

Our Purpose

Taking care of a pet comes with many small responsibilities — feeding routines, vaccinations, medicines, vet visits and important reminders. Pet Care Buddy brings these everyday tasks together so pet parents can keep their pet's care organized and easy to manage.
